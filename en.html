02 / OPEN SOURCE

From one idea
to many physical possibilities

FatQat is SpaceQat's open-source foundation, connecting quantum program expression, compilation, and simulation in one workflow.

View on GitHub
FatQatAn open entry point
for diverse physical constraints
  1. 01WRITE

    Express an idea

    Use a unified program representation to turn quantum algorithms into code that can be verified and shared.

  2. 02COMPILE

    Adapt to physical constraints

    Bring connectivity, movement, fusion, and other hardware constraints from diverse qubit systems into compilation and control.

  3. 03SIMULATE

    Validate through simulation

    Choose the right modeling depth for each problem, from general simulation to physical dynamics.

  4. 04CONNECT

    Connect more possibilities

    We continue to explore more physical approaches and will progressively open interfaces to real hardware across different modalities.
